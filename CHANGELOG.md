# Changelog

Each release has a `## <version>` section; the release workflow uploads only the section matching
`mod.version` in gradle.properties.

## Unreleased

- Added an opt-in GemRender development runtime on every node through `-Pgemrender.run=true`.
- Crop items and both sapling variants have generated models; welded iridium plates use the Space iridium tint.

- Plushie compression, DuoDec note effects and the socket angel centrepiece follow Space's rendering and gameplay.
- Orbit remains at noon. Solar arms read live foreign-energy connections and publish changes to clients.
- Saving solar panels preserves their recorded arm mask without loading neighboring chunks during shutdown.
- Planetary bed and respawn rules preserve usable beds and stored respawn points.
- Satellite tooltips include authored Dyson-relay and War payload masses outside loaded worlds.
- Transporter controls, particle providers and item-model redirects use the same public APIs on all supported nodes.
- The electrolyser's inventory and held-item transforms are an authored item definition.
- Pumps accept the planetary soils listed by Space 1.7.
- Oil and gas ore items from every body supply their authored fluid contents without an empty container.
